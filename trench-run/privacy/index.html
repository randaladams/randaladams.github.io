Trench Run Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Trench Run game for Android ("the app") uses, and why. The app is published by Adams Elite ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact support@adamselite.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your scores, badges and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The free edition shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to serve and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Purchases of Trench Run Pro are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your scoreboard, badges, lifetime statistics and settings (such as sound and control preferences) on your device so they're there next time you play. We don't receive this information. You can erase it at any time with Clear All on the Scores & Badges screen, or by uninstalling the app. If you use Android's built-in backup, your device may include this data in its backup to your Google account, under Google's terms.

Device features the app uses

  • Motion sensors (accelerometer) to steer the ship. Readings are used live for control only and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration for haptic feedback during play.
  • Text-to-speech: spoken warnings use your device's own text-to-speech engine.
  • Internet access is used to load ads in the free edition and to process purchases through Google Play.

Advertising (free edition)

The free edition shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve, personalize (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the app asks for your consent or choices before ads are personalized, using Google's consent tools. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Trench Run Pro does not show ads.

Purchases

Trench Run Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells the app whether you own Pro. We don't receive your name, payment card or billing details. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles purchase information.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help address it.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone, because we don't collect it. Information collected by Google for ads or purchases is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Security and retention

Data the app stores stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. We don't keep copies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
