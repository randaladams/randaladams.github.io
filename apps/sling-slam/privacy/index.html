Sling Slam Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Sling Slam app for Android (com.adamselite.slingslam, "the app") uses, and why. The app is published by adamselite.com ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact support@adamselite.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to serve and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your high score, your last 20 runs (score, level reached and whether you used a continue), your sound setting and whether you finished the tutorial on your device so they're there next time. We don't receive this information. You can erase it by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you use Android's built-in backup, your device may include this data in its backup to your Google account, under Google's terms.

Device features the app uses

  • Vibration for short haptic feedback, such as when a rock hits your wall.
  • Internet access is used only to load ads.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner at the top of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to continue after your shield runs out. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help address it.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't collect it. Information collected by Google for ads or purchases is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date.
