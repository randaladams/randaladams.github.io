Tut's Trumpets Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Tut's Trumpets app for Android (com.randaladams.tuttrumpets, "the app") uses, and why. The app is published by adamselite.com ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact support@adamselite.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • The app doesn't save any personal information.
  • The app doesn't connect to the internet, shows no ads and has no purchases.

Information stored on your device

The app doesn't save any personal information. Android may keep its normal app settings, which are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Device features the app uses

  • Links: the info screen can open web pages (Wikipedia and the BBC) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • No internet access: the app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help address it.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't collect it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date.
