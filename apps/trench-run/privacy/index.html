Trench Run Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Trench Run app for Android (com.adamselite.trenchrun, "the app") uses, and why. The app is published by adamselite.com ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact support@adamselite.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to serve and measure those ads, as described below.
  • The optional Trench Run Pro purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your scoreboard, badges, lifetime statistics and settings (such as sound and control preferences) on your device so they're there next time. We don't receive this information. You can erase it with Clear All on the Scores & Badges screen, or by uninstalling the app. If you use Android's built-in backup, your device may include this data in its backup to your Google account, under Google's terms.

Device features the app uses

  • Motion sensors (accelerometer) to steer. Readings are used live for control only and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration for haptic feedback during play.
  • Text-to-speech: spoken warnings use your device's own text-to-speech engine.
  • Internet access is used only to load ads in the free version and to process purchases through Google Play.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner on menu screens, an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to continue after a crash. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalized.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Once you own Trench Run Pro, the app no longer shows ads.

Purchases

The optional Trench Run Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play to remove ads and unlock Pro features. Google processes the payment and tells the app only whether you own it, so it can be restored after a reinstall. We don't receive your name, payment card or billing details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help address it.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't collect it. Information collected by Google for ads or purchases is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date.
