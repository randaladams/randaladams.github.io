Bingo2Playlist Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Bingo2Playlist app for Android (com.randaladams.bingo2playlist, "the app") uses, and why. The app is published by adamselite.com ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact support@adamselite.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows no ads and has no purchases. Card photos stay on your phone unless you turn on Claude reading.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your saved playlists, any unfinished batch and your settings (including an Anthropic API key, if you enter one) on your device so they're there next time. We don't receive this information. You can erase it with the delete button on each saved playlist, by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you use Android's built-in backup, your device may include this data in its backup to your Google account, under Google's terms.

Device features the app uses

  • Camera: the Camera button opens your phone's own camera app to photograph a card. The app has no camera permission of its own.
  • Photos: the Gallery button uses Android's photo picker, so the app sees only the photos you choose.
  • Text recognition on your phone: by default, cards are read on your device with Google ML Kit. Your photos and the text read from them stay on your phone. ML Kit does send Google performance and usage metrics (such as device model, OS version and processing time), as described in the ML Kit terms.
  • Sharing and saving: playlists leave your phone only when you share or save them, to the app or place you choose.
  • Links: Find and the TuneMyMusic button open Spotify or the TuneMyMusic website. Those services have their own privacy policies.
  • Internet access is used only if you turn on Claude reading (below), and by Google ML Kit for the metrics described above.

Optional Claude reading

In Settings you can choose to have cards read by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, using your own Anthropic API key. When this is on, each card photo you add is sent directly from your phone to Anthropic's API to be read; it does not pass through us. Your API key is stored only on your phone. Anthropic handles these photos under its own terms and privacy policy. This option is off unless you turn it on.

Using it in a web browser

Bingo2Playlist also runs in a web browser at adamselite.com/apps/bingo2playlist/play/. It works the same way as the app: your photos are processed in your browser, and your playlists, unfinished batch and settings are saved only in your browser's own storage on your device. Nothing is sent to us. You can erase them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

The first time you read a card, the page loads the free Tesseract.js text-recognition software and its English language data from public code servers such as jsDelivr. Those servers see a normal web request, such as your IP address, but not your photos; reading happens in your browser. If you turn on Claude reading, photos go from your browser directly to Anthropic as described above. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log technical details of requests to load the page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help address it.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't collect it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date.
