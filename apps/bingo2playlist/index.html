Bingo2Playlist

Photograph your music bingo cards and turn every song on them into one playlist.

Open in your browser

Works on phones and computers. On a phone, Camera opens your camera; on a computer, pick photos of your cards.

Download for Android (APK) Source code

Installing: open this page on your Android phone (Android 9 or newer) and tap Download. Open the downloaded file and, when Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. This app isn't on Google Play; new versions install over the old one and keep your playlists.
  • Start a batch and snap card after card: the camera reopens after each photo.
  • Song titles and artists are read from each card, even through dauber ink.
  • Songs from every card merge into one list, with duplicates removed automatically.
  • Fix any title, add or remove songs, then save the playlist on your phone.
  • Share or save it as a CSV for importing into Spotify (with TuneMyMusic or Soundiiz), a text list or an M3U file.
  • Tap Find next to any song to open it in Spotify.
