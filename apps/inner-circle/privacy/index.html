Inner Circle Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Inner Circle app for Android (com.example.innercircle, "the app") uses, and why. The app is published by adamselite.com ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact support@adamselite.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app doesn't connect to the internet, shows no ads and has no purchases.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your scores, best run, level progress, stats and settings on your device so they're there next time. We don't receive this information. You can erase it with the reset option in the game, or by uninstalling it. If you use Android's built-in backup, your device may include this data in its backup to your Google account, under Google's terms.

Device features the app uses

  • No internet access: the app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help address it.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't collect it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date.
